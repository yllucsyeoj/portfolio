I work with founders and operators on the questions that sit between the data team and the commercial team: why isn't this converting, where is the margin actually going, which customers are worth keeping, what should we charge. The work is diagnosing the business problem from the numbers, then helping build what addresses it.

Available for remote interim and contract work.

## Selected work

Each piece below is a real analysis. Some client work is anonymised; self-directed studies link to the full code.

### Commercial and customer analytics

- **[A membership programme worth NT$16.7M, or NT$1.7M?](pieces/pet-retention):** a pet-services chain's enrolment opportunity looked ten times bigger than it was. Found the honest, actionable number and the retention problem underneath it. **Live.**
- **[Where a swimwear brand's revenue actually lives](pieces/swimwear-retention):** segmented 221 wholesale accounts and found that 6% of them carry a third of revenue while the mainstream base quietly shrinks. Ends in a different playbook per segment. **Live.**
- **[A clinic that was quietly becoming a different business](pieces/vet-clinic):** revenue up 57% and visits tripled, but the average ticket halved. A surgical practice had turned into a chronic-care one, and its future rested on a cohort bet nobody had named. **Live.**

### Decision science and logistics

- **[The £138,750 hidden inside a warehouse rent](pieces/hines-logistics):** a vehicle-routing and geodemographic study for Hines that put a number on the logistics cost buried in where a warehouse sits. Published as a research paper. **Live.**
- **[Finding store sites with graph theory instead of a map](pieces/woodfine-site-selection):** modelled every retailer in the US as a graph to find where a Walmart, a Costco and a Home Depot cluster together, ranking 9,189 candidates down to 41. **Live.**
- **[Scoring every site in a country as a location](pieces/site-scoring-engine):** a multi-criteria model that ranks a national network of fuel-and-logistics sites against roughly 38 signed criteria, from port growth to competitor pricing gaps, across six countries. **Live.**
- **[Rebuilding a city as a map of its buildings](pieces/city-as-buildings):** joined property records, address registers, map data and an aggregate mobility panel into a building-level picture of who lives where, for a real estate investor. Aggregate only, no individual identified. **Live.**

### Marketplace and growth

- **Cold-start supply from regulatory signals:** used public licensing data to seed the supply side of a B2B marketplace before it had sellers. *(writeup in preparation)*

### Market research and segmentation

- **[Who actually buys a pre-planned pet funeral](pieces/pet-survey):** a survey of 267 pet owners run with real statistical discipline, where the obvious segments (age, gender) predicted nothing and a behavioural chain predicted everything. **Live.**

### Signals and robustness

- **[Insiders selling into strength](pieces/insider-buybacks):** a study of 9,009 officer stock sales across 48 US companies, and the signal that survived its own stress tests. Full code and 84 offline tests in the repo. **Live.**
- **[A finding that survives some tests and fails others](pieces/scale-and-signal):** a self-directed study of German district-level data where a demographic signal holds under regional controls and collapses under spatial ones. The piece is about which of the two answers to trust, and why. **Live.**
